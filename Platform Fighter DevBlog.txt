Knockback information:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CxvdO_kkXmY
Knockback formula
	(((((p / 10 + pd / 20) * 200 / w + 100 * 1.4) + 18) * s) + b) * r

	p - current percent of the target Character
	d - damage of the dealt attack (d = d1 * 0.3(StalingBonus) * 0.3(FreshnessBonus))
	w - character's weight
	s - knockback scaling (rate at which knockback increases with damage)
	b - base knockback
	r - Error term (rage, crounch cancelling, etc)

	Launch speed = 0.3 * knockback
		Decreases by 0.015 every frame until the horizontal speed reaches zero or the character hits the ground
			Air traction values are disabled during this
		DI changes launch speed
		Tumble only occurs when the knockback value is over a certain threshold
	Rage = 1 + (p - 35 / 115 * 0.15)
Knockback is a vector in which the magnitude is the knockback value and the angle is the angle in which the hitbox sends
